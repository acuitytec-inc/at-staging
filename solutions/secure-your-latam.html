Document + Selfie

From government-issued IDs to biometric liveness, we offer a seamless, secure solution for verifying identities.

Powered by cutting-edge AI technology for real-time capture, combined with our advanced risk analysis engine, you can confidently build trust and mitigate fraud, all while streamlining your verification process.

Capture

The user captures or uploads an image of their government-issued identity document, providing real-time data capture with intuitive, secure document scanning technology.

Extract

AcuityTec’s advanced OCR engine intelligently extracts key data points from the document, including name, birthdate, and document details, ensuring accuracy and speed.

Verify

Our AI-driven verification technology cross-checks the document’s format and data against global standards to authenticate its validity and match the user’s identity securely.

Results

AcuityTec delivers pass, warning, or fail risk recommendations with a risk score, while securely storing documents and data for management and ease of future reference.
